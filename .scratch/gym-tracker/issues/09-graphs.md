# 09: Graphs

**What to build:** Each exercise has its own page with a weight-over-time graph (weighted exercises only) and a reps-per-set graph per session. Incomplete sessions show as hollow points. An overview page lists all active exercises with their current weight (or "bodyweight"), the date of their last increase and a small trend line, and tapping one opens its page. The session screen shows a compact version of the current exercise's progress chart.
